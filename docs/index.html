GAIA

Severe Weather Detection for East Tennessee

What is GAIA?

GAIA is an early-warning system for severe weather in East Tennessee. It combines satellite data, radar, lightning detection, terrain modeling, and hazmat facility awareness to issue alerts with hours of lead time—before storms form.

Comparison to National Weather Service

  • NWS tornado false alarm rate: 75% — GAIA: 18.5%
  • NWS average lead time: 13 minutes — GAIA: 176 minutes

Coverage

10 East Tennessee counties: Knox, Sevier, Blount, Greene, Hamblen, Hawkins, Washington, Grainger, Sullivan, Anderson.

TRI (Toxics Release Inventory) awareness: 87 hazmat facilities in the region. GAIA factors proximity to chemical facilities when issuing warnings.
